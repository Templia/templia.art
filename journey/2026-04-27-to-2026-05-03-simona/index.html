The Jaguar at the Heart of the Sacred Count

You were born under one of the most powerful nawales of the Tzolkin, Simona. 7 Ix means that the Jaguar — the keeper of the earth's deep mysteries and the divine feminine — walks through you at the very center of the sacred calendar. Tone 7 is the column of light at the heart of the count; it is the most spiritually potent of all thirteen tones, and it asks for inner work, reflection, and the quiet authority of one who walks between worlds. The Jaguar does not need to roar. She moves silently, sees in the dark, and arrives without warning. To carry 7 Ix is to be granted the rare role of priestess-witness — to perceive what others cannot, to hold space for the sacred without dramatizing it, to know the earth's silent magic. During your stay, the calendar will return your own tone of 7 to you on Day 4 — the central day of your journey will share the mystical-center number that defines you. The Jaguar will recognize her own pulse in the heart of these seven days.

Discover

Templia’s integration into Mayan cultural preservation aligns with several initiatives in Tulum aimed at promoting Mayan heritage.

Nearby, two of the most significant expressions of this effort — Parque del Jaguar and the Museo Regional de la Costa Oriental — offer direct access to the land, history, and worldview that shaped this region.

Museo Regional de la Costa Oriental (Mureco)

Located within Parque del Jaguar, the museum presents the story of the Caribbean Maya from prehistoric times to the present. Its exhibition halls display original artifacts, ceremonial objects, and murals uncovered during the Tren Maya works, providing cultural and historical context to the landscapes surrounding Tulum. Open daily: 9:00–17:00

Parque del Jaguar

A protected natural reserve encompassing jungle, archaeological zones, and Caribbean coastline. The park connects walking trails, bike paths, miradors, and public beaches, allowing visitors to move through spaces once considered sacred in Maya cosmology — where nature, history, and daily life converge.
